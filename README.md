One Emoji, One Smile — the single emoji most likely to make a stranger smile: **🫂 People Hugging**. Two people embracing is the most direct warmth gesture there is — kind at first glance, universal, platonic, and deliberately chosen: the field is saturated with 🤗, 🥰, 🫶 and 🌻, while a hug between two people is absent from it.
